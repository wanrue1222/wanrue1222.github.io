"Flaneuse" in 21st Century Shenzhen

Independent Research Oberlin College

A qualitative study examining the female-friendliness of Shenzhen's urban design through a feminist lens. Drawing on the historical figure of the Flaneuse, this research investigates three sites — Shenzhen Metro, Shenzhen Bay Park, and lesbian bar "Jiquan" — through ethnographic observation, surveys, and interviews. Findings reveal tensions between strong physical infrastructure and persistent gaps in women's subjective safety and access.
